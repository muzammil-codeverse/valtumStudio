# ADR 0006: Structured content over a page builder

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-07-12

## Context

Future editorial content must be reusable across desktop and mobile presentations.

## Decision

Model future editable content as structured domain data rather than a free-form
page-builder document.

## Alternatives considered

A generic page builder, hard-coded marketing copy, and duplicated content per
presentation.

## Consequences

Future validation, SEO, and presentation composition can work from a consistent content
source.

## Follow-up implications

No CMS models or content CRUD are introduced before their approved phase.
