# ADR 0010: White-first light-only presentation

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-07-12

## Context

The V1 public visual direction is explicitly white-first and light-only.

## Decision

Set the global canvas to `#ffffff`, declare light color-scheme behavior, and defer final
visual tokens to the design phase.

## Alternatives considered

Dark mode, warm-white surfaces, dark presentation sections, and speculative brand
tokens.

## Consequences

The placeholder is accessible and visually neutral without pre-committing the final
design system.

## Follow-up implications

Do not introduce dark-mode support or final brand styling without a later approved
design decision.
