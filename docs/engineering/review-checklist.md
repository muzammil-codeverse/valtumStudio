# Review checklist

- Is the change within the approved phase and module boundary?
- Are types, errors, and configuration explicit and safely validated?
- Are server/client boundaries and secrets protected?
- Are tests meaningful and deterministic?
- Are accessibility and performance effects appropriate for the change?
- Are dependencies necessary, compatible, and licensed appropriately?
- Do documentation, environment examples, and operations instructions match?
- Are known risks, migrations, and compatibility effects recorded?
